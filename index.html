Bengali Voice Recording

Research project: offline symptom recognition for people with limited access to healthcare.

What you will do

You will see short Bengali sentences describing health symptoms, one at a time. Read each one aloud in your natural voice, listen back, and press Submit. There are 113 sentences; each takes a few seconds. You can stop any time and come back later on the same device.

What we collect

  • Your voice recordings of these sentences.
  • The name you enter below. It is used only to organise recordings and is kept private by the research team.
  • Basic technical information (browser type, recording length).

How it is used

Recordings are stored privately in the research team's Google Drive and used only to test and improve speech recognition for this project. They will not be published.

Your choices

  • Taking part is voluntary. You can skip any sentence, especially those marked sensitive topic.
  • You can stop at any time and delete everything you submitted using the Withdraw option at the bottom of the page.
  • The sentences describe symptoms but are not about you; you are reading a script.

Questions: contact the person who sent you this link.

How it works

  1. Read the sentence shown in large Bengali text. The English line under it is only a translation.
  2. Press ● Record, say the sentence in your natural voice, then press ■ Stop.
  3. Listen back. If it sounds clear, press Submit. If not, press Re-record.
  4. Not comfortable with a sentence? Press Skip. Sentences marked sensitive topic are always fine to skip.
  5. Your progress is saved on this device. You can close the page and come back later to continue.

For best results: a quiet room, phone about a hand's width from your mouth, normal speaking speed. Small changes in wording are fine; say it the way you naturally would.
